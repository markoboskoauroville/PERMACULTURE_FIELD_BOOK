# HANDOFF — PERMACULTURE_FIELD_BOOK

**State: complete and published.** Fourth in the Mantra field-book series.

## Decisions worth not re-litigating

1. **The evidence chapter is the spine of the book.** Do not soften it in
   either direction. Permaculture went forty years without yield data; there
   is now some and it is cautiously positive. Both halves stay.
2. **"Comparable to conventional and organic" is the finding.** Not
   "outperforms". The movement routinely inflates this and the book
   deliberately does not.
3. **The ancestors get a full chapter** — Smith, Howard, Yeomans, Fukuoka —
   because permaculture's real contribution is synthesis and transmission,
   which is a smaller and more defensible claim than the one usually made.
4. **Swales are criticised at length**, specifically on karst and on steep
   slopes. This is the single most useful correction in the book for a
   Croatian reader and should not be trimmed for being negative.
5. **No invented site data for Kukljica.** See README.
